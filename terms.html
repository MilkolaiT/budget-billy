Budget Billy - Terms of Use

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Budget Billy is a personal budgeting application intended for private use by its owner.

Purpose

Budget Billy provides tools for personal financial tracking, budgeting, transaction analysis and categorization.

Bank account access

Access to bank account information is performed through authorized Open Banking services and requires explicit authorization from the account holder.

Financial information

Information provided by Budget Billy is for personal informational purposes and does not constitute financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

Availability

The availability and accuracy of banking information may depend on third-party financial institutions and Open Banking providers.

Use

Budget Billy is currently operated as a private personal project and is not offered as a commercial financial service to the public.
