Budget Billy - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Budget Billy is a personal budgeting application intended for private use by its owner.

Data accessed

Budget Billy may access financial account information, including account balances and transaction data, through authorized Open Banking providers.

Purpose

Financial data is accessed solely for personal budgeting, transaction analysis, categorization, financial tracking and related personal finance features.

Authorization

Bank account information is accessed only after explicit authorization from the account holder through the relevant Open Banking authentication process.

Data sharing

Budget Billy does not sell financial or personal data to third parties.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials, authentication keys and financial information.

Contact

Questions regarding privacy and data protection can be sent to the data-protection email address registered for Budget Billy.
